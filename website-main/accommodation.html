Hotels near Campus

The hotels listed below are representative options in the immediate vicinity of Tongji University's Siping Campus.

Over 30 additional quality hotels rated four stars and above are available within a five-kilometer radius of the campus, easily accessible via Tongji University Station on Metro Line 10.

Negotiated rates and a discount portal will be announced.
